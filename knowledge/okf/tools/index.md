# Tool

* [VirtualBox](virtualbox.md) - Runs the VMs on your computer.
* [Vagrant](vagrant.md) - Creates, starts and deletes the VMs from a Vagrantfile per method.
* [Ansible](ansible.md) - Runs the install from your computer, for either method.
* [cephadm](cephadm.md) - Bootstraps and manages Ceph in the podman method.
* [Podman](podman.md) - Runs every Ceph daemon as a container in the podman method.
